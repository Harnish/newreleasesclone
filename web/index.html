🔔 Hear about it your way

Browser push notifications, an email the moment a release lands, or a daily digest of yesterday's releases. You choose per project.

🔗 Signed webhooks

Send new releases to your own automation, chat bot or CI, with optional HMAC-SHA256 signatures so you can trust what arrives.

🧪 Prereleases only if you want them

Alphas, betas and release candidates are detected and hidden by default. Turn them on for the projects where you want an early look.

📝 Release notes in one click

Expand any version to read its notes, or open a project to see its last ten releases with dates. No visiting each project page.

🏷 Tags and sorting

Group projects with your own tags, filter by them, and sort by latest release, date added or name.

💾 Back up repos to your GitLab

Mirror GitHub and GitLab projects to your own GitLab on a daily, weekly or monthly schedule, plus an auto-generated "Awesome" list of everything you track.

📥 Bring your list with you

Already on newreleases.io? Import your projects with your API key. Paste any project URL and the platform is detected for you.

🔒 Self-hosted, your data

Runs on your own server. Project icons are cached by the server, so your browser never contacts third parties. Works the same on phone and desktop.
